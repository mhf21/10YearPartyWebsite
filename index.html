Getting there

Everything you need to plan flights and get around once you land.

Flying in

Fly into Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL). Halekulani is about 9 miles away, roughly 20–40 minutes by car depending on traffic. Late April into May is a popular time to visit, so book flights early.

Getting around

Rideshare and taxis are easy from the airport, and Waikīkī itself is very walkable. If you plan to explore the North Shore or Kailua, rent a car for a day or two. Hotel parking in Waikīkī is often charged nightly.

Weather & packing

Early May is warm and mostly dry, typically in the high 70s to low 80s °F, with ocean breezes. Pack reef-safe sunscreen (Hawai‘i bans sunscreens with oxybenzone and octinoxate), a light layer for evenings, and something nice for Saturday.

Arriving from the mainland

No passport needed for U.S. travelers. Everyone arriving in Hawai‘i completes an agricultural declaration, which you can usually do online or on the plane.
